Playstack privacy policy

App: Playstack (Android, package com.vlr.playstack) · Publisher: Valeri Yordanov · Effective 21 October 2026

Playstack is a collection of games you can play offline. This page explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. You don't need an account to use Playstack.

Data that stays on your device

Your game progress, scores, settings, recently played games and hint counts are stored only on your device. We don't collect them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Data handled by services we use

Some features use services from Google and GitHub. They receive data directly from your device, under their own privacy policies:

ServiceWhat it's forData involved
Google AdMob The small ads under the menus, and the optional video ads you can watch for a hint or booster. Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. Ads are personalised only if you consent.
Google User Messaging Platform Asking for and storing your consent choices (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). Your consent choices, stored on your device.
Firebase Analytics Understanding how the app is used, to improve it. App usage events, device and app information, an app-instance ID. Respects your consent choices.
Firebase Crashlytics Finding and fixing crashes. Crash details, device state and an installation ID.
Firebase Remote Config Adjusting app settings (for example, ad settings) without an update. An app-instance ID.
Google Play Billing The optional one-time Premium purchase. Google handles the payment. We only receive whether Premium was bought; we never see your payment details.
GitHub (GitHub Pages) The banners at the top of each tab and their settings. Your IP address and browser information, as in any web request.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · GitHub Privacy Statement

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it, the app asks before ads or analytics use your data. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads).
  • No ads: Premium, a one-time purchase, removes all ads.
  • Offline: every game works without an internet connection; without one, no data is sent.

Children

Playstack is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

How long data is kept

Data held by Google and GitHub is kept according to their policies and our settings in their consoles. Analytics and crash data are used only in aggregate, to improve the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Because we don't keep your data on our own servers, most requests are best handled through the services above, but you can always contact us and we'll help. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Bulgaria, the Commission for Personal Data Protection).

Future features

Online play and rankings are planned. Registering for them will be optional. If they collect new data, this policy will be updated before they launch.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
